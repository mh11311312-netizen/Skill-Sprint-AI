# Account Opening and KYC Procedure

## 1. Purpose
Applies to: All employees

1.1 This SOP explains the steps for opening a new customer account in line with the AML and KYC Compliance Policy (POL-07).

## 2. Customer Onboarding
Applies to: Sales Executive, Customer Service Officer

2.1 Staff must complete the Account Opening Form with all mandatory fields before submission.
2.2 Staff must verify the CNIC of the customer through NADRA verification before opening the account.
2.3 Staff must obtain the signature of the customer on the paper Account Opening Form.
2.4 Staff should explain the account fee schedule to the customer.

## 3. Approval
Applies to: Sales Executive, Branch Manager

3.1 Every new account must be approved by the Branch Manager before activation.
3.2 Branch Managers must approve or return account applications within 2 working days.

## 4. High-Risk Customers
Applies to: Sales Executive, Branch Manager, Compliance Officer

4.1 Accounts for high-risk customers must be referred to the Compliance Officer before approval.
4.2 Compliance Officers must complete the high-risk review within 2 working days.

## 5. Training
Applies to: Sales Executive, Customer Service Officer, Branch Manager

5.1 Staff in scope must demonstrate opening a test account in the training system within 14 days of joining.
