# Cash Handling and Vault Procedure

## 1. Purpose
Applies to: All employees

1.1 This SOP defines the controls for handling cash at branch counters and in the vault.

## 2. Teller Cash Limits
Applies to: Cash Teller

2.1 Cash Tellers must not hold more than PKR 2,000,000 in their counter drawer at any time.
2.2 Excess cash must be transferred to the vault immediately using the vault transfer slip.
2.3 Cash Tellers must count cash in front of the customer and under CCTV coverage.
2.4 Cash Tellers must balance their cash drawer at the end of each working day.

## 3. Vault Control
Applies to: Branch Manager, Operations Coordinator

3.1 The vault must be opened only under dual control by two authorised staff members.
3.2 Vault keys and combination codes must be held by different people.
3.3 Operations Coordinators must reconcile the vault balance with the core banking system weekly.

## 4. Shortages and Excesses
Applies to: Cash Teller, Branch Manager

4.1 Any cash shortage or excess above PKR 1,000 must be reported to the Branch Manager immediately.
4.2 Branch Managers must record shortages in the incident register and inform Operations on the same day.

## 5. Counterfeit Notes
Applies to: Cash Teller

5.1 Cash Tellers must check every high-value note using the counterfeit detection machine.
5.2 Cash Tellers should retain suspected counterfeit notes and issue a receipt to the customer.

## 6. Training
Applies to: Cash Teller, Branch Manager, Operations Coordinator

6.1 Staff in scope must demonstrate the cash drawer balancing procedure under supervision within the first week of joining.
