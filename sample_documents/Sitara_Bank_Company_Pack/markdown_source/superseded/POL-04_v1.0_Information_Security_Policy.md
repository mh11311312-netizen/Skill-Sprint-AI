# Information Security Policy

## 1. Purpose
Applies to: All employees

1.1 This policy defines how Sitara Bank protects its information, systems and customer data.

## 2. Security Awareness
Applies to: All employees

2.1 All employees must complete the Information Security Basics module within the first week of joining.
2.2 All employees must sign the Acceptable Use acknowledgement on Day 1.
2.3 Employees must report any suspected security incident to the IT Service Desk within 1 hour of noticing it.

## 3. Passwords and Access
Applies to: All employees

3.1 Passwords must be at least 8 characters long and include upper-case letters, numbers and symbols.
3.2 Employees must change their passwords every 90 days.
3.3 Employees must not share their passwords or access cards with anyone, including colleagues and managers.
3.4 Employees must enable multi-factor authentication on their email account within the first week of joining.

## 4. Devices and Removable Media
Applies to: All employees

4.1 Personal USB drives and external storage devices must not be connected to bank computers.
4.2 Employees must lock their screen whenever they leave their desk.
4.3 Employees should store work files only on the approved network drive.

## 5. Email and Phishing
Applies to: All employees

5.1 Employees must report suspicious emails using the Report Phishing button in Outlook.
5.2 Employees must not open attachments from unknown senders.

## 6. Privileged Access
Applies to: IT Support Engineer

6.1 IT Support Engineers must use separate administrator accounts for privileged tasks.
6.2 IT Support Engineers must remove the system access of leavers within 24 hours of receiving an HR exit notice.
6.3 IT Support Engineers must demonstrate the secure device-setup checklist within 30 days of joining.
