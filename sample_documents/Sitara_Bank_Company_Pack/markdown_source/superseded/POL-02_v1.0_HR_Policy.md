# HR Policy

## 1. Purpose
Applies to: All employees

1.1 This policy sets out the employment terms that apply to all employees of Sitara Bank.

## 2. Probation
Applies to: All employees

2.1 All new employees serve a probation period of 90 days from their date of joining.
2.2 Employees must complete all mandatory onboarding modules before the end of probation.
2.3 During probation, employees are not entitled to any paid leave.
2.4 Employees must complete a probation review meeting with their reporting manager within 90 days of joining.

## 3. Working Hours
Applies to: All employees

3.1 Official working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday to Friday, including a one-hour break.
3.2 Employees must obtain prior approval from their reporting manager for any overtime.

## 4. Performance Management
Applies to: All employees

4.1 Employees must agree written performance objectives with their reporting manager within 30 days of joining.
4.2 Employees should request informal feedback from their reporting manager every month.

## 5. HR Operations
Applies to: HR Executive

5.1 HR Executives must verify the educational certificates of every new joiner within 14 calendar days of the new joiner's start date.
5.2 HR Executives must maintain employee records only in the HR Information System and must not store personnel files on personal devices.
5.3 HR Executives must complete the HR Information System training within the first week of joining.
5.4 HR Executives should conduct an exit interview with every resigning employee.

## 6. Team Supervision
Applies to: Team Leader, Branch Manager

6.1 Supervisors must approve or reject leave requests within 2 working days.
6.2 Supervisors must conduct a monthly one-to-one meeting with each team member.
6.3 Supervisors must complete the People Management Essentials module within 30 days of joining.
