# Data Privacy Policy

## 1. Purpose
Applies to: All employees

1.1 This policy governs how personal data of customers and employees is collected, used, stored and destroyed.

## 2. Personal Data Handling
Applies to: All employees

2.1 Employees must access customer personal data only when it is required for their assigned duties.
2.2 Employees must complete the Data Privacy Essentials module within 14 days of joining.
2.3 Customer data must not be sent to personal email addresses or messaging apps.

## 3. Customer-Facing Staff
Applies to: Customer Service Officer, Sales Executive, Cash Teller, Branch Manager

3.1 Customer-facing staff must verify the identity of the customer using at least two identifiers before sharing account information.
3.2 Customer-facing staff must not write customer account numbers on paper notes.
3.3 Customer-facing staff should use the screen privacy filter provided at the counter.

## 4. Data Retention
Applies to: Compliance Officer, Operations Coordinator, Data Analyst

4.1 Customer transaction records must be retained for 7 years from the date of account closure.
4.2 Records past their retention period must be destroyed using the approved secure disposal process.

## 5. Data Breach
Applies to: All employees

5.1 Any suspected data breach must be reported to the Compliance Officer within 24 hours.
5.2 Compliance Officers must notify the regulator of a confirmed breach within 72 hours. (Applies to: Compliance Officer)

## 6. Analytics
Applies to: Data Analyst

6.1 Data Analysts must use anonymised or masked data sets for analysis and reporting.
6.2 Data Analysts must not export customer-level data outside the analytics environment.
6.3 Data Analysts may use aggregated data in presentations without further approval.
