# Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose
Applies to: All employees

1.1 This policy explains the types of leave available and how leave is requested and approved.

## 2. Annual Leave
Applies to: All employees

2.1 Confirmed employees are entitled to 20 working days of annual leave per calendar year.
2.2 Employees must apply for annual leave at least 7 days in advance through the HR portal.
2.3 Employees must not take more than 10 consecutive working days of annual leave without Department Head approval.
2.4 Unused annual leave may be carried forward up to a maximum of 10 days.

## 3. Sick Leave
Applies to: All employees

3.1 Employees must inform their reporting manager before 10:00 AM on any day they cannot come to work due to sickness.
3.2 Sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days must be supported by a medical certificate.

## 4. Mandatory Leave
Applies to: Cash Teller, Branch Manager, Operations Coordinator

4.1 Staff in these roles must take at least 3 consecutive working days of leave each year as a fraud-control measure.
4.2 During mandatory leave, the duties of the employee must be performed by another authorised staff member.

## 5. Leave Approval
Applies to: Team Leader, Branch Manager

5.1 Approvers must check team coverage before approving overlapping leave requests.
5.2 Approvers should avoid approving leave for more than 30% of a team at the same time.

## 6. Related Guidance
Applies to: All employees

6.1 Employees may refer to the HR and Leave FAQ (FAQ-01) for common questions; where the FAQ differs from this policy, this policy prevails.
