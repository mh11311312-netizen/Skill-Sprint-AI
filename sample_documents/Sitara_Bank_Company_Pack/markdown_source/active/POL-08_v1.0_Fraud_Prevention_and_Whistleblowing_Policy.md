# Fraud Prevention and Whistleblowing Policy

## 1. Purpose
Applies to: All employees

1.1 This policy explains how the bank prevents, detects and responds to fraud.

## 2. Fraud Awareness
Applies to: All employees

2.1 All employees must complete the Fraud Awareness module within 30 days of joining.
2.2 Employees must report suspected fraud through the whistleblowing channel or to the Compliance Officer.
2.3 Employees who report concerns in good faith are protected from retaliation.

## 3. Whistleblowing
Applies to: All employees

3.1 Employees may report concerns anonymously through the confidential hotline.
3.2 Employees must not attempt to investigate suspected fraud on their own.

## 4. Segregation of Duties
Applies to: Finance Associate, Operations Coordinator, Cash Teller

4.1 The same employee must not both initiate and approve a payment.
4.2 Staff must use the maker-checker process for all payments and cash adjustments.

## 5. Investigation
Applies to: Compliance Officer

5.1 Compliance Officers must log every fraud report in the case management system within 24 hours.
5.2 Compliance Officers must escalate fraud cases above PKR 500,000 to the Head of Compliance.
