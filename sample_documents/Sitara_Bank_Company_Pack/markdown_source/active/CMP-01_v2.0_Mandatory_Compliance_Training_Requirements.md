# Mandatory Compliance Training Requirements

## 1. Purpose
Applies to: All employees

1.1 This document lists the compliance training modules for employees and the order in which they are taken.

## 2. Modules for All Employees
Applies to: All employees

2.1 All employees must complete Information Security Basics within the first week of joining.
2.2 All employees must complete Data Privacy Essentials within 14 days of joining.
2.3 All employees must complete Fraud Awareness within 30 days of joining.
2.4 All employees must complete Anti-Harassment Awareness within 30 days of joining.
2.5 All employees must pass each mandatory module assessment with a minimum score of 70%.

## 3. Role-Based Modules
Applies to: Branch Manager, Customer Service Officer, Sales Executive, Cash Teller, Compliance Officer, Operations Coordinator

3.1 Staff in scope must complete AML and KYC Fundamentals within 30 days of joining.

## 4. Prerequisites
Applies to: All employees

4.1 Information Security Basics must be completed before Data Privacy Essentials.
4.2 Data Privacy Essentials must be completed before any customer-data access training.
4.3 AML and KYC Fundamentals must be completed before the Account Opening practical assessment.
4.4 The Complaint Handling module must be completed before the Escalation Procedure module.

## 5. Refresher Training
Applies to: All employees

5.1 Employees must complete refresher training for all mandatory modules every 12 months.
5.2 Employees who fail an assessment twice must be referred to their reporting manager for review.
