# New Joiner Process Manual

## 1. Purpose
Applies to: All employees

1.1 This manual describes the onboarding journey of a new employee from Day 1 to Day 90.

## 2. Day 1
Applies to: All employees

2.1 New joiners must report to the HR desk at 9:00 AM on Day 1.
2.2 New joiners receive their laptop and system access on Day 1.
2.3 New joiners must attend the welcome orientation session on Day 1.

## 3. Week 1
Applies to: All employees

3.1 New joiners must complete the mandatory security and conduct modules within the first week of joining.
3.2 New joiners should meet at least three colleagues from other departments within the first week of joining.

## 4. First 30 Days
Applies to: All employees

4.1 New joiners must complete their role-specific learning plan milestones within 30 days of joining.
4.2 New joiners must attend a check-in meeting with their reporting manager within 30 days of joining.

## 5. First 60 and 90 Days
Applies to: All employees

5.1 New joiners must complete all practical assessments within 60 days of joining.
5.2 New joiners must complete the final onboarding assessment within 90 days of joining.
5.3 New joiners may request a mentor from the HR team at any time during onboarding.

## 6. Branch New Joiners
Applies to: Cash Teller, Customer Service Officer, Branch Manager, Sales Executive

6.1 Branch new joiners must complete a branch security walkthrough on Day 1.
6.2 Branch new joiners must not handle customer cash or customer data until AML and KYC Fundamentals is completed.
