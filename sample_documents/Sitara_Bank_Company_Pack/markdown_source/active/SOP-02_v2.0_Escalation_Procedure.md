# Escalation Procedure

## 1. Purpose
Applies to: All employees

1.1 This SOP defines when and how customer issues and operational incidents are escalated.

## 2. Escalation Levels
Applies to: Customer Service Officer, Branch Manager, Operations Coordinator, Team Leader

2.1 Level 1 issues are handled by the front-line employee who receives them.
2.2 Unresolved customer complaints must be escalated to the Branch Manager within 24 hours.
2.3 Issues unresolved at branch level must be escalated to the Regional Office within 3 working days.
2.4 Every escalation must be recorded in the CMS together with the reason for escalation.

## 3. Operational Incidents
Applies to: Operations Coordinator, Team Leader

3.1 Operations Coordinators must escalate system outages affecting customers to IT Support within 15 minutes.
3.2 Team Leaders must inform the Branch Manager of any incident affecting more than 10 customers.
3.3 Team Leaders should hold a short debrief with the team after every major incident.

## 4. Training
Applies to: Customer Service Officer, Branch Manager, Operations Coordinator, Team Leader

4.1 Staff in scope must complete the Escalation Procedure module within 14 days of joining.
4.2 Staff in scope must demonstrate the escalation workflow in a role-play scenario within 30 days of joining.
