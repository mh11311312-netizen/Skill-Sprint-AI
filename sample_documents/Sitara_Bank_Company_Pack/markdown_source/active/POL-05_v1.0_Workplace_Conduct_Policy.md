# Workplace Conduct Policy

## 1. Purpose
Applies to: All employees

1.1 This policy describes the standards of professional behaviour expected from every employee.

## 2. Professional Behaviour
Applies to: All employees

2.1 Employees must treat customers and colleagues with respect and courtesy.
2.2 Employees must not accept gifts worth more than PKR 5,000 from customers or vendors.
2.3 Employees must declare every gift received in the Gift Register within 3 working days.
2.4 Employees must acknowledge the Code of Conduct on Day 1.

## 3. Harassment and Discrimination
Applies to: All employees

3.1 Employees must not engage in any form of harassment or discrimination.
3.2 Employees should report harassment to HR or through the confidential helpline.
3.3 All employees must complete the Anti-Harassment Awareness module within 30 days of joining.

## 4. Conflict of Interest
Applies to: All employees

4.1 Employees must declare any conflict of interest to HR within the first week of joining.
4.2 Employees may undertake outside part-time work only with written approval from HR.

## 5. Social Media
Applies to: All employees

5.1 Employees must not post confidential bank information on social media.
5.2 Marketing Executives must use only the approved bank accounts when posting official content. (Applies to: Marketing Executive)
