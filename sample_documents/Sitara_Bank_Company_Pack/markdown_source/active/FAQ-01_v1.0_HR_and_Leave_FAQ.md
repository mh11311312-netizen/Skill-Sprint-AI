# HR and Leave FAQ

## 1. Can I take leave during probation?
Applies to: All employees

1.1 No. Employees on probation cannot take any paid leave.

## 2. How early do I need to apply for annual leave?
Applies to: All employees

2.1 You should apply for annual leave at least 7 days in advance.

## 3. What are the office hours?
Applies to: All employees

3.1 Office hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.

## 4. How do I get my payslip?
Applies to: All employees

4.1 Payslips are available on the HR portal on the last working day of each month.
4.2 You must keep your bank account details updated on the HR portal.

## 5. Who do I contact for HR questions?
Applies to: All employees

5.1 Contact the HR Service Desk at extension 2200 or hr.desk@sitarabank.example.

## 6. Can I work from home?
Applies to: All employees

6.1 Work from home may be allowed for non-customer-facing roles with Department Head approval.

## 7. Do I need a medical certificate for sick leave?
Applies to: All employees

7.1 A medical certificate is required for sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days.

## 8. When must onboarding training be finished?
Applies to: All employees

8.1 All mandatory onboarding modules must be completed within 60 days of joining.
