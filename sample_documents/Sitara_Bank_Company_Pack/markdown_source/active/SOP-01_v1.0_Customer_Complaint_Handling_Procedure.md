# Customer Complaint Handling Procedure

## 1. Purpose
Applies to: All employees

1.1 This SOP describes how customer complaints are received, resolved and closed.
1.2 In this SOP, the terms complaint and grievance have the same meaning.

## 2. Receiving Complaints
Applies to: Customer Service Officer, Cash Teller, Sales Executive, Branch Manager

2.1 Staff must log every customer complaint in the Complaint Management System (CMS) at the time it is received.
2.2 Staff must give the customer a complaint reference number.
2.3 Staff should apologise for the inconvenience without admitting liability.

## 3. Resolution
Applies to: Customer Service Officer

3.1 Customer Service Officers must acknowledge each complaint within 24 hours.
3.2 Customer Service Officers must resolve standard complaints within 5 working days.
3.3 Unresolved complaints must be escalated to the Branch Manager after 48 hours.
3.4 Customer Service Officers must demonstrate complaint logging in the CMS training environment within the first week of joining.

## 4. Closure
Applies to: Customer Service Officer, Branch Manager

4.1 Complaints must be closed only after the customer confirms resolution or 7 days pass without a response.
4.2 Branch Managers must review all complaints closed in their branch every week.
4.3 Staff may offer a goodwill gesture of up to PKR 2,000 with Branch Manager approval.
