# Marketing Content Approval Procedure

## 1. Purpose
Applies to: All employees

1.1 This SOP describes how marketing content is created, reviewed and approved before publication.

## 2. Content Creation
Applies to: Marketing Executive

2.1 Marketing Executives must follow the Brand Guidelines (MKT-GD-01) for all external content.
2.2 Marketing Executives must not publish interest rates or offers that have not been approved by the Product team.
2.3 Marketing Executives should prepare content at least 10 working days before the campaign launch.
2.4 Marketing Executives must include the regulatory disclaimer on every advertisement for a financial product.

## 3. Approval
Applies to: Marketing Executive, Compliance Officer

3.1 All external marketing content must be reviewed by the Compliance Officer before publication.
3.2 Compliance Officers must complete marketing content reviews within 3 working days.
3.3 Approved content must be stored in the Marketing Content Library with its approval reference.

## 4. Customer Data in Marketing
Applies to: Marketing Executive

4.1 Marketing Executives must include only customers who have given marketing consent in campaign lists.
4.2 Marketing Executives must request campaign data from the Data Analytics team using the data request form described in SOP-08.

## 5. Training
Applies to: Marketing Executive

5.1 Marketing Executives must complete the Responsible Marketing module within 30 days of joining.
