# Role Descriptions

## 1. Branch Manager
Applies to: Branch Manager

1.1 The Branch Manager leads all branch operations, branch staff and customer service at the branch.
1.2 The Branch Manager must hold a daily morning briefing with branch staff.
1.3 The Branch Manager must complete the Branch Leadership Programme within 90 days of joining.

## 2. Customer Service Officer
Applies to: Customer Service Officer

2.1 The Customer Service Officer handles customer enquiries, complaints and service requests.
2.2 The Customer Service Officer must achieve a customer satisfaction score of at least 85% in quarterly surveys.
2.3 The Customer Service Officer should learn the full product range of the bank within 30 days of joining.

## 3. Sales Executive
Applies to: Sales Executive

3.1 The Sales Executive acquires new customers and sells deposit and card products.
3.2 The Sales Executive must meet the monthly new-account target agreed with the Branch Manager.
3.3 The Sales Executive may approve new accounts with an expected balance of up to PKR 500,000 without Branch Manager approval.

## 4. HR Executive
Applies to: HR Executive

4.1 The HR Executive supports recruitment, onboarding, employee records and exits.
4.2 The HR Executive must conduct the onboarding orientation for new joiners every Monday.
4.3 The HR Executive must complete the Pakistan Labour Law Basics module within 30 days of joining.

## 5. Finance Associate
Applies to: Finance Associate

5.1 The Finance Associate processes payments, reconciliations and month-end reporting.
5.2 The Finance Associate must complete the core banking finance module within the first week of joining.
5.3 The Finance Associate should gain proficiency in advanced spreadsheet functions within 60 days of joining.

## 6. Operations Coordinator
Applies to: Operations Coordinator

6.1 The Operations Coordinator oversees branch back-office processing and vault operations.
6.2 The Operations Coordinator must monitor daily end-of-day processing and report exceptions.
6.3 The Operations Coordinator must complete the Business Continuity Plan training within 60 days of joining.

## 7. IT Support Engineer
Applies to: IT Support Engineer

7.1 The IT Support Engineer resolves hardware, software and access issues for bank staff.
7.2 The IT Support Engineer must be available for on-call support one week per month.
7.3 IT Support Engineers working the night shift are exempt from the daily morning briefing.

## 8. Compliance Officer
Applies to: Compliance Officer

8.1 The Compliance Officer monitors adherence to regulatory requirements and internal policies.
8.2 The Compliance Officer must complete the Certified AML Specialist preparation course within 90 days of joining.
8.3 The Compliance Officer must submit a monthly compliance report to the Head of Compliance.

## 9. Marketing Executive
Applies to: Marketing Executive

9.1 The Marketing Executive plans and runs marketing campaigns across digital and branch channels.
9.2 The Marketing Executive must track campaign performance and report results every month.
9.3 The Marketing Executive may attend external marketing workshops with manager approval.

## 10. Data Analyst
Applies to: Data Analyst

10.1 The Data Analyst prepares dashboards, reports and analysis for management decisions.
10.2 The Data Analyst must produce the weekly branch performance dashboard.
10.3 The Data Analyst must complete the SQL and data visualisation skills assessment within 30 days of joining.

## 11. Cash Teller
Applies to: Cash Teller

11.1 The Cash Teller handles cash deposits, withdrawals and counter transactions.
11.2 The Cash Teller must process each customer cash transaction accurately within a service time of 5 minutes.
11.3 The Cash Teller must complete a shadowing period with a senior Cash Teller within the first week of joining.

## 12. Team Leader
Applies to: Team Leader

12.1 The Team Leader supervises a team of 5 to 10 staff in operations or customer service.
12.2 The Team Leader must assign daily work to team members and monitor productivity.
12.3 The Team Leader must complete the People Management Essentials module within 30 days of joining.
