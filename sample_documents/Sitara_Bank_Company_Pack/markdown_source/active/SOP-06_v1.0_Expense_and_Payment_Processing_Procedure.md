# Expense and Payment Processing Procedure

## 1. Purpose
Applies to: All employees

1.1 This SOP describes how expenses are claimed and how vendor payments are processed.

## 2. Expense Claims
Applies to: All employees

2.1 Employees must submit expense claims with original receipts within 30 days of incurring the expense.
2.2 Claims without receipts are not reimbursed.

## 3. Payment Processing
Applies to: Finance Associate

3.1 Finance Associates must verify vendor details against the approved vendor master before processing any payment.
3.2 Finance Associates must process approved vendor invoices within 7 working days.
3.3 Payments above PKR 1,000,000 must be approved by the Chief Financial Officer.
3.4 Finance Associates must not process a payment to a vendor whose bank details changed in the last 5 days without call-back verification.
3.5 Finance Associates must demonstrate a complete maker-checker payment cycle in the finance training system within 30 days of joining.

## 4. Month-End Close
Applies to: Finance Associate

4.1 Finance Associates must complete bank reconciliations by the 5th working day of each month.
4.2 Finance Associates should prepare variance explanations for differences above 5%.

## 5. Branch Expenses
Applies to: Branch Manager

5.1 Branch Managers must approve branch petty cash expenses up to PKR 25,000 per month.
5.2 Branch Managers may delegate petty cash approval to a Team Leader in writing.
