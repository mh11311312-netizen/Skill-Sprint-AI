# Employee Handbook

## 1. About Sitara Bank
Applies to: All employees

1.1 Sitara Bank Ltd. is a retail bank headquartered in Karachi with 40 branches across Pakistan.
1.2 The core values of the bank are Integrity, Customer First, Accountability and Security.
1.3 This handbook summarises key expectations; detailed rules are set out in the relevant policies and SOPs.

## 2. Joining Formalities
Applies to: All employees

2.1 Every new employee must sign the Employee Handbook acknowledgement form on Day 1.
2.2 New employees must submit copies of their educational certificates to HR within the first week of joining.
2.3 New employees must collect their employee ID card from the HR desk on Day 1.
2.4 New employees should meet their reporting manager within the first week of joining to agree on role expectations.

## 3. Working Hours and Attendance
Applies to: All employees

3.1 Standard office hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday to Friday.
3.2 Employees must record attendance using the biometric system at the start and end of each working day.
3.3 Employees may request flexible timing with written approval from their reporting manager.

## 4. Dress Code
Applies to: All employees

4.1 Employees must follow the business formal dress code on all customer-facing days.
4.2 Employees should refer to the Workplace Conduct Policy (POL-05) for detailed behavioural standards.

## 5. Use of Bank Resources
Applies to: All employees

5.1 Employees must use bank equipment only for official purposes.
5.2 Employees may use the staff cafeteria and prayer rooms during scheduled breaks.
5.3 Employees must return all bank property on their last working day.
