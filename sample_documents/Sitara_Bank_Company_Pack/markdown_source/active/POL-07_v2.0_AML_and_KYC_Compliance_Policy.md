# AML and KYC Compliance Policy

## 1. Purpose
Applies to: All employees

1.1 This policy sets out the Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Know Your Customer (KYC) controls of the bank.

## 2. AML Training
Applies to: Branch Manager, Customer Service Officer, Sales Executive, Cash Teller, Compliance Officer, Operations Coordinator

2.1 Staff in scope must complete the AML and KYC Fundamentals module within 14 days of joining.
2.2 Staff in scope must pass the AML assessment with a minimum score of 80%.

## 3. Customer Due Diligence
Applies to: Sales Executive, Customer Service Officer, Branch Manager, Cash Teller

3.1 Staff must verify the CNIC of the customer through the NADRA verification system before opening any account.
3.2 Staff must obtain source-of-income documents for accounts expected to receive more than PKR 1,000,000 per month.
3.3 Enhanced due diligence must be applied to politically exposed persons (PEPs).

## 4. Suspicious Activity
Applies to: Sales Executive, Customer Service Officer, Branch Manager, Cash Teller, Operations Coordinator

4.1 Staff must report any suspicious transaction to the Compliance Officer on the same working day.
4.2 Staff must not inform the customer that a suspicious activity report has been filed.

## 5. Cash Thresholds
Applies to: Cash Teller, Branch Manager

5.1 If a single cash deposit exceeds PKR 2,000,000, the Cash Teller must obtain Branch Manager approval before processing it.
5.2 Branch Managers must review the daily large-cash transaction report before close of business.

## 6. Compliance Monitoring
Applies to: Compliance Officer

6.1 Compliance Officers must file Suspicious Transaction Reports with the Financial Monitoring Unit within 7 days of detection.
6.2 Compliance Officers must perform monthly sample testing of KYC files.
6.3 Compliance Officers should maintain a watch list of high-risk customers.
