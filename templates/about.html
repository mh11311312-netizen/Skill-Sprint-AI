Sitara Bank Ltd. · Internal onboarding system

New staff onboarding, built from the bank's own documents.

SkillSprint reads our approved policies, SOPs and role descriptions, and prepares a personal onboarding plan for every new joiner: what to learn, what to complete, and by when. Every rule in a plan points back to the exact document and section it came from, and every plan is checked before anyone relies on it.

How it works

Two separate checks stand behind every plan: one writes it, the other verifies it.

  1. 1
    Documents are uploaded

    HR and Compliance upload policies, SOPs, FAQs and role descriptions as PDF or Word files. Old versions are kept but not used.

  2. 2
    Rules are extracted

    The system turns every "must", "should" and "may" into a requirement, linked to the roles it applies to. Conflicting documents are settled by the bank's precedence rules.

  3. 3
    A plan is drafted

    A language model drafts a personal plan for the employee's role and experience, using only the approved clauses.

  4. 4
    The plan is checked

    A separate rule-based check compares the draft against the requirement list: missing items, wrong deadlines, invented rules and outdated sources are flagged.

  5. 5
    A reviewer signs off

    A trainer or reviewer approves, edits or rejects the plan. Every decision is kept in the audit trail.

  6. 6
    The employee learns

    The new joiner works through modules, checklists, tasks and quizzes, and managers can see progress by role and department.

Security

Uploaded documents are treated as information, never as instructions. Text that tries to give the system orders, or is hidden in white or tiny print, is quarantined and never reaches the language model. Access depends on your role, passwords are stored encrypted, and every sign-in and decision is logged.
